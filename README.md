## **SHE’S EVERYWHERE: Mapping Female Population**  

### **Project Overview**  
This project visualizes the distribution of the female population across the world using **Tableau**. It presents insights on the **percentage of female population by country, region, and trends over time**, helping to understand demographic patterns globally.  

### **Key Insights & Visualizations**  
🔹 **Percentage of Female World Population (2022):** Shows that women make up **49.74%** of the global population.  
🔹 **Percentage of Women by Region:** Asia leads with **51.47%**, followed by Africa and the Americas.  
🔹 **Most Densely Female Populated Regions (2022):** A geographical heatmap representation of the female population distribution.  
🔹 **Trends in Female Population (Top 3 Countries):** Analyzes **China, India, and the U.S.**, showing **historical growth trends**.  
🔹 **Yearly Additional Population & Fertility Rate:** Displays **birth rates and yearly population increments** across major countries.  
🔹 **Populous Country Trends:** Tracks population growth for **India, China, and the U.S.** from **1975 to 2020**.  
🔹 **Female Population by Country:** **China and India** have the highest female population, followed by the **U.S. and Indonesia**.  
🔹 **Global Population Distribution Map:** A geographical breakdown of female population density worldwide.  

### **Technologies Used**  
✅ **Tableau** – Data visualization & interactive dashboard  
✅ **Data Sources** – World Bank, United Nations datasets  
✅ **Excel / CSV** – Data preprocessing  

### **Impact & Applications**  
📌 Helps policymakers in **gender-based resource allocation**  
📌 Supports research on **female population distribution trends**  
📌 Useful for **economic and social development studies**
